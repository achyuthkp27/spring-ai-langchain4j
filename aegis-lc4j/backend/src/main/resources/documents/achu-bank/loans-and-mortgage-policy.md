# Achu Bank — Loans & Mortgage Policy (DOC-ACHU-LOAN-006)

## Personal Loans
Amounts from **$2,000 to $50,000**, terms **12 to 60 months**. No early
repayment penalty on personal loans. Rate quotes are valid for **30 days**.

## Mortgages
Maximum loan-to-value is **80%** (or **90% for first-time buyers** with
mortgage insurance). Early repayment of a fixed-rate mortgage within the first
**3 years** incurs a **2% prepayment charge** on the amount repaid early.

## Hardship Support
Customers in verified financial hardship may defer repayments for up to
**3 months** per rolling 24-month period without a credit-file impact.

## Staff Conduct
Operations staff must NOT provide investment, product-selection, or borrowing
advice. Rate and eligibility questions are answered from policy only; customers
seeking advice are referred to a licensed advisor.
