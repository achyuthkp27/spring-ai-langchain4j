# Achu Bank — Card Services Policy (DOC-ACHU-CARD-003)

## Daily Limits
Debit cards: ATM withdrawals up to **$1,000 per day**, point-of-sale purchases up
to **$5,000 per day**. Contactless (tap) payments are capped at **$250 per tap**;
above that the terminal requires chip-and-PIN.

## International Use
Cards must be enabled for international use before travel (self-service in the
app or by staff request). A **2% foreign transaction fee** applies to debit
purchases abroad; premium credit cards are exempt.

## Lost, Stolen or Compromised Cards
Staff must freeze the card IMMEDIATELY on report — freezing is protective and
reversible. Customers have **zero liability** for unauthorized card transactions
reported within **60 days** of the statement date.

## Replacement Cards
Standard replacement: **$5 fee**, delivered in **5-7 business days**. Expedited
replacement: **$25 fee**, delivered in **2 business days**. The replacement fee
is waived when the card was compromised through no fault of the customer.

## PIN Security
Three consecutive incorrect PIN attempts block the card automatically. PIN reset
requires identity verification per the KYC policy.
