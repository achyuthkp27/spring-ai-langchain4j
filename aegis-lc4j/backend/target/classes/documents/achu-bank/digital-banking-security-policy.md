# Achu Bank — Digital Banking & Security Policy (DOC-ACHU-SEC-007)

## Authentication
Online banking requires a passphrase of **at least 12 characters**. Biometric
login (Face/Touch ID) is opt-in per device. **Three failed login attempts**
lock digital access for **30 minutes**; further failures require staff-assisted
identity verification.

## What The Bank Never Asks
Achu Bank NEVER asks customers for their password, full PIN, or a one-time
passcode (OTP) — by phone, SMS, or email. Any such request is fraud and must be
reported.

## Fraud Response
The fraud hotline operates **24/7** with a **2-hour response SLA** for account
takeover reports. Confirmed account takeover: all digital channels are frozen,
cards are blocked, and new credentials are issued within **24 hours**.

## Devices
Customers can deregister a lost device from the app or via staff; deregistration
takes effect immediately.
