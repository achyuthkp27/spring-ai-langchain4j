# Achu Bank — Overdraft Policy (DOC-ACHU-OD-010)

## Coverage
Overdraft protection is **opt-in only**. Without opt-in, transactions that
would overdraw the account are declined with no fee.

## Fees
**$30 per overdraft item**, charged at most **3 times per day**. No fee when
the account is overdrawn by **$10 or less**. Fees are refunded automatically if
the account is brought positive by **9:00 PM ET the next business day**.

## Alternatives
A free savings-sweep link transfers from a linked Achu Savings account in $50
increments with no fee.

## Extended Overdraft
An account continuously overdrawn for **30 days** is referred to collections
and digital access may be restricted.
