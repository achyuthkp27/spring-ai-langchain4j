# Achu Bank — Interest & Deposits Policy (DOC-ACHU-DEP-008)

## Savings Interest (APY)
Paid monthly, tiered by balance:
- Under $10,000: **1.50% APY**
- $10,000 to $100,000: **2.25% APY**
- Over $100,000: **3.00% APY**

## Fixed Deposits (Certificates)
- 6 months: **3.50%**
- 12 months: **4.00%**
- 24 months: **4.50%**
Early withdrawal forfeits **90 days of interest** on the amount withdrawn.

## Deposit Insurance
Deposits are insured up to **$250,000 per depositor, per ownership category**.
