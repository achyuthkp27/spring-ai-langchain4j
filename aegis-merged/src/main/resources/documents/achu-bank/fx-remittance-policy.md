# Achu Bank — Foreign Exchange & Remittance Policy (DOC-ACHU-FX-009)

## Exchange Rates
Retail FX conversions carry a margin of at most **1.5% over the mid-market
rate**. A quoted rate is locked for **15 minutes**.

## Remittance Limits
International remittances: **$10,000/day for Tier 1** customers,
**$50,000/day for Tier 2+**. Higher one-off limits require compliance approval.

## Screening
All cross-border payments pass sanctions and AML screening automatically.
Flagged payments are held for compliance review, resolved within
**1 business day**; staff must not release a held payment themselves.
