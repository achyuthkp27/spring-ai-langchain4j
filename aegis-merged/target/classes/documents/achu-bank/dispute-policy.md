# Achu Bank — Card Dispute Policy (DOC-ACHU-DP-001)

## Filing Deadlines
Customers must file a dispute for a card transaction within **120 days** of the
transaction posting date. Disputes for recurring subscription charges must be
filed within **60 days** of the disputed billing date.

## Provisional Credit
For disputes classified as UNAUTHORIZED_TRANSACTION or FRAUD_SUSPECTED, Achu Bank
issues a provisional credit within **2 business days** of case creation, up to a
maximum of **$5,000** without senior approval. Amounts above $5,000 require
approval from a Disputes Team Lead.

## Duplicate Charges
For DUPLICATE_CHARGE disputes, the case handler must first confirm the merchant
descriptor and timestamp match. Confirmed duplicates are refunded within
**3 business days**. No provisional credit is issued for duplicate charges.

## Chargeback Representment
If a merchant contests a chargeback, Achu Bank has **45 days** to submit
representment evidence. Failure to respond forfeits the chargeback.
