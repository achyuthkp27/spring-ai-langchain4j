# Achu Bank — Minor & Custodial Accounts Policy (DOC-ACHU-MINOR-013)

## Eligibility
Custodial accounts (UTMA/UGMA-style) are available for account holders under
**18**, opened jointly with a parent or legal guardian as custodian. No
monthly fee applies to custodial checking or savings.

## Custodian Authority
The custodian has full transaction authority until the minor reaches the
**age of majority (18, or 21 if elected at account opening)**, at which point
the account converts automatically to a standard individual account in the
former minor's name — the custodian's access ends on that date without
further action required from either party.

## Card Access
A debit card may be issued to the minor starting at **age 13**, with a
custodian-set daily spending limit (default **$100/day**, adjustable by the
custodian at any time). Minors cannot hold a credit card, request a
provisional credit, or open a dispute case independently — those actions
require the custodian.

## KYC for Minors
The minor's identity is verified via school ID or birth certificate plus the
custodian's own Tier 1+ KYC; no independent credit check is performed on the
minor.

## Conversion & Closure
At conversion to majority, Achu Bank sends notice to both parties **30 days**
in advance. The custodian may close the account any time before conversion
with no early-closure fee (the standard 90-day early-closure fee does not
apply to custodial accounts).
