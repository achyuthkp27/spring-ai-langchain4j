# Achu Bank — Complaints & Grievance Policy (DOC-ACHU-COMP-011)

## Service Levels
Every complaint is acknowledged within **1 business day** and resolved within
**15 business days**. Complex cases may take up to **30 business days**, with a
written status update at day 15.

## Escalation Path
Agent → Team Lead → Customer Advocacy (ombudsman liaison). Customers may also
contact the external banking ombudsman at any time.

## Redress
Fees caused by verified bank error are refunded automatically. Staff may add a
goodwill credit up to **$25** at their own discretion; larger amounts require
Team Lead approval.
