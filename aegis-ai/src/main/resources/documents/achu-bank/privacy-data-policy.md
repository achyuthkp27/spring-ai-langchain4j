# Achu Bank — Privacy & Data Policy (DOC-ACHU-PRIV-012)

## Data Use
Achu Bank does not sell customer data. Data is shared only with payment
processors, credit bureaus as required for the service, and authorities under
legal obligation.

## Customer Rights
Marketing opt-out requests take effect within **10 days**. Data access and
deletion requests are fulfilled within **30 days**; regulatory retention rules
(e.g. KYC documents kept 7 years after closure) take precedence over deletion.

## Breach Notification
Customers affected by a confirmed data breach are notified within **72 hours**
of confirmation, with remediation steps and dedicated support.
