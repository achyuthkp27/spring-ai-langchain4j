# Achu Bank — Accounts & Fees Policy (DOC-ACHU-ACCT-004)

## Account Types & Monthly Fees
- **Everyday Checking:** $12/month, waived with a $1,500 minimum daily balance
  or a monthly direct deposit of $500+.
- **Achu Savings:** no monthly fee.
- **Business Checking:** $25/month, waived above a $10,000 average balance.

## Statements
E-statements are free. Paper statements cost **$3 per statement cycle**.
Statement history is available for 7 years.

## Dormancy & Closure
An account with no customer-initiated activity for **12 months** is flagged
dormant; after **24 months** a **$5/month dormancy fee** applies until
reactivation. Account closure is free after 90 days from opening; closures
within the first 90 days incur a **$25 early-closure fee**.

## Joint Accounts & Beneficiaries
Joint accounts require consent of ALL holders for closure or removal of a
holder. Beneficiary (payable-on-death) designations can be updated by the
account holder at any time with identity verification.

## Deceased Customer Accounts
On notification of death, accounts are frozen pending a certified death
certificate and estate documentation. Direct debits are stopped; incoming
deposits are held. Release to the estate follows compliance review.
