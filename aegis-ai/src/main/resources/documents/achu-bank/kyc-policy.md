# Achu Bank — KYC & Onboarding Policy (DOC-ACHU-KYC-002)

## Identity Verification Tiers
- **Tier 1 (basic):** government ID + proof of address. Transaction limit $10,000/month.
- **Tier 2 (enhanced):** Tier 1 + source-of-funds documentation. No monthly limit.
- **Tier 3 (institutional):** enhanced due diligence, beneficial-ownership disclosure.

## Enhanced Due Diligence Triggers
EDD is mandatory when any of the following apply: a customer is a politically
exposed person (PEP), the account originates from a high-risk jurisdiction, or
cumulative transactions exceed **$50,000** in a rolling 30-day window.

## Document Retention
KYC documents are retained for **7 years** after account closure, encrypted at
rest with customer-managed keys. Access is restricted to the Compliance team.
