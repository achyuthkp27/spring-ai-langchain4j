# Achu Bank — Payments & Transfers Policy (DOC-ACHU-PAY-005)

## Transfer Types & Timelines
- Internal (Achu-to-Achu): instant, free, 24/7.
- ACH transfers: **1-2 business days**; cutoff **5:00 PM ET**.
- Domestic wire: **$20 fee**, same-day if submitted before **3:00 PM ET**.
- International wire: **$40 fee**, **1-3 business days**, subject to sanctions
  screening.

## Daily Transfer Limits
Aligned with KYC tiers: **Tier 1: $10,000/day. Tier 2: $100,000/day.**
Tier 3 (institutional) limits are set per relationship agreement.

## Standing Orders & Direct Debits
Standing orders are free to set up and modify. A returned direct debit
(insufficient funds) incurs a **$15 return fee**; three returns in 90 days
trigger a service review.

## Cheques
Cheque deposits clear in **2 business days**. Deposits over **$5,000** may be
held up to **5 business days**. Stop-payment requests cost **$30** and are valid
for 6 months.
